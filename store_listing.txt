3. Full Description:
   RouteFinderKE is your essential travel companion for navigating Nairobi's public transport system.
   Never get lost or overcharged again! Quickly look up Matatu route numbers, start points, destinations, and major stops across the city.

   Features:
   • Comprehensive Route Catalog: Access details for popular routes like 7C, 23, 105, 4W, 33B, and more.
   • Detailed Route Info: View major stops/landmarks for every route to know exactly where to board or alight.
   • Fare Estimates: Stay informed with estimated fare ranges to help you plan your daily commute budget.
   • Material Design: Enjoy a clean, fast, and user-friendly interface designed for Kenyan commuters.
   • Lightweight: Small app size that won't take up much space on your device.
